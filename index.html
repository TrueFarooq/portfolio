Understanding the Practice

Building strong, stable relationships requires organization, structure, and mutual dedication. In household management, daily duties such as cooking, laundry, and maintaining the home can be rotated among adult partners, ensuring no single individual bears the entire domestic workload.

Economic Benefits: Sharing a unified home creates significant financial advantages. Pooling resources and sharing household bills significantly reduces individual overhead costs, decreasing overall stress and eliminating the need for each adult to work excessive hours.

Childcare & Guidance: Having multiple supportive adults involved in raising and educating children brings diverse strengths, ensuring well-rounded instruction, nurturing, and guidance.

The Gospel of Jesus Christ

The perfect structural order of life originates directly from God. God the Father is the supreme head over all creation. Jesus Christ, the Son, is subject to the Father, and the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son. Within the created earthly order, man is positioned under God's authority, woman under man's authority, and children under their parents' guidance.

God created everything in perfection. However, human sin broke this relationship. Because God loved humanity, He entered the world in human flesh through His Son, Jesus Christ. Jesus lived a completely sinless life and willingly died on the cross, taking upon Himself the punishment for our sins.

He was buried and rose again from the dead on the third day. Following His resurrection, He was seen by over 400 people before ascending into heaven.

On Pentecost, God sent the Holy Spirit to dwell inside all who place their trust in Jesus. Eternal life and salvation are received entirely as a gift through faith in what Christ did on the cross—not by any good works or moral self-righteousness.
